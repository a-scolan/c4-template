To avoid context errors after switching projects, re-anchor the session to the new project before touching the C3 view: first re-run a narrow `search-element` for a known container or component in the target project and confirm the project id; then re-run `read-element` on the target container to confirm its kind, children, and `defaultView`; then re-run `query-graph` with `children` to confirm the exact C3 boundary; finally re-run `find-relationships` for the edges you plan to show. In this repo, the safe mental model is that model definitions live in files such as `projects/spec-showcase/components.c4`, `projects/spec-showcase/containers.c4`, or `projects/template/system-model.c4`, while the actual C3 view edit belongs in the target project's view file such as `projects/spec-showcase/component-views.c4` or `projects/template/system-views.c4`. Do not reuse ids across projects just because the names look similar: `api_container.*` in `spec-showcase` and `mySystem.api` in `template-project` are different contexts.
