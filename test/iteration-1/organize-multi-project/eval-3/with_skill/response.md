Do not put everything into the shared area. Separate reusable technical conventions from reusable business domains. A good structure is: `projects/shared/` for shared specs, tags, kinds, relationship definitions, and shared images; domain folders such as `projects/payments/`, `projects/customer/`, or `projects/inventory/` for real business models and views. If some business models are genuinely reused across multiple projects, give them their own clearly named project rather than hiding them inside shared specs. That keeps the repo understandable without turning it into a maze. The rule of thumb is simple: if it is a reusable definition or convention, it belongs in `shared`; if it is an actual system, actor, container, or domain model, it belongs in a project. Keep the number of top-level projects small and meaningful, keep includes relative, and keep cross-project dependencies explicit and minimal.
