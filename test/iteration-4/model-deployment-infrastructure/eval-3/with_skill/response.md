No. Those technologies usually belong on the logical relationships in the system model, not on duplicated deployment edges for normal application traffic. Once deployed apps use instanceOf, LikeC4 inherits the relationships automatically into deployment views. Even though the shared deployment spec defines deployment relationship kinds like https and tcp, use them sparingly for infrastructure-specific connections that are not part of normal business traffic, such as monitoring, backups, bastion access, log shipping, or replication.
