The first local conventions to understand are: shared taxonomy by level in spec-context.c4, spec-containers.c4, spec-components.c4, and spec-code.c4; the split between business or model relationships in spec-global.c4 and deployment or infrastructure relationships in spec-deployment.c4; PascalCase public names with Category_Subtype patterns; tags as semantics and filtering rather than replacements for kinds; and the rule that technology belongs on the system-model relationship. The real sources of truth are the shared .c4 spec files; projects/shared/SPEC_CHEATSHEET.md is only a convenience summary. After that, hand off to c4-modeling-process so the actual modeling follows the repository C1 to C2 to C3 structure instead of importing UML or ArchiMate structure directly.
