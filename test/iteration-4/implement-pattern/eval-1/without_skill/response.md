Apply the explicit queue pattern from the shared spec: model the publisher as a Container_Api, RabbitMQ as a Container_Queue, and the worker as a processing container such as Container_ProcessingServer. Use async relationships for the publish and consume legs, with technology AMQP. The mistake to avoid is drawing a direct calls relationship from the API to the worker, or otherwise bypassing the queue, because that makes the flow look synchronous and removes the decoupling.
