The root cause is abstraction leakage plus duplication. The shared specs separate business/model relationships from deployment/infrastructure relationships: `projects/shared/spec-global.c4` defines business types like `calls`, and `projects/shared/SPEC_CHEATSHEET.md` says to put interaction technology on the system-model relationship; `projects/shared/spec-deployment.c4` reserves `https` and similar types for infrastructure-specific deployment links.

The clean fix is to model the app interaction once in `model {}` with a business relationship such as `webapp -[calls]-> api` and set the technology to `HTTPS` there. Keep deployment focused on `instanceOf`, and only add deployment-level `-[https]->` links when they represent extra infrastructure connectivity that is not just the same logical app call repeated. The cheat sheet also says to add a port only when it is non-default for the protocol.
