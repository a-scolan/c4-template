Choose ProdEuApiVm, not EuProdApiVm. The cleanest convention is to treat the region as part of the environment name, for example ProdEu and ProdUs, and then keep the normal VM formula unchanged: Environment plus Service plus Vm. That gives you ProdEuApiVm, ProdEuWorkerVm, ProdEuDatabaseVm, ProdUsApiVm, ProdUsWorkerVm, and ProdUsDatabaseVm. For zones, keep the standard names inside each environment: Dmz, AppTier, ProcTier, and DataTier. Only prefix zones if you truly need global uniqueness outside the environment scope; if so, stay consistent and use ProdEuAppTier, not EuProdAppTier.
